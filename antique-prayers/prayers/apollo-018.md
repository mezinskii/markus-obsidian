## Оригинал





O lucis almae rector et caeli decus,
qui alterna curru spatia flammifero ambiens
da, Phoebe, veniam, si quid inlicitum tui
videre vultus.

## Русский




## Английский

Your pardon I do desire, Apollo, if you see in my actions what is forbidden; for it was by the will of another that I revealed hidden things of the earth.
