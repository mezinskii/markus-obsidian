## Оригинал





non pacem petimus, superi: date gentibus iras,
nunc urbes excite feras; coniuret in arma
mundus, Achaemeniis decurrant Medica Susis.

## Русский




## Английский

Gods above, we do not pray for peace. Grant rage onto the nations. May You now arouse the cities; bring forth the whole world in arms to war.
