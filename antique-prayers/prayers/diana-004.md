## Оригинал





Incola Tifatae venatibus incluta virgo,
haec Latona tuis statuit miracula templis,
cunctis notus homo silvarum cultor et ipse,
laudibus inmensis vitae qui servat honorem.

## Русский




## Английский

Resident of Tifata, Glorious Virgin of hunters, in this place, Daughter of Latona, was Your temple established, renowned by the whole of mankind, Dweller of the Forest, and here as well, an unending praise of life for You who preserves honor.
