## Английский

What may a poet ask in his prayers of You, Apollo? What can he say as he pours a libation of new wine to You?
May Apollo grant that I enjoy good health and a sound mind, and, I pray, when I grow old, may He grant me a strife-free life, a clear mind and a lyre beside me with which to sing His praises.
