## Оригинал





Haec tibi pro nato plena dat laetus acerra,
Phoebe, Palatinus munera Parthenius.

## Русский




## Английский

An offering I gladly give You, Phoebus, a box of fragrant incense, from Palatinu Parthenius on behalf of his son.
