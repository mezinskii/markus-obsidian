## Оригинал





Te te laborum socia et adiutrix precor,
belligera Pallas, cuius in laeva ciet
aegis feroces ore saxifico minas;
adsit Lycurgi domitor et rubri maris,
tectam virente cuspidem thyrso gerens,
geminumque numen Phoebus et Phoebi soror.

## Английский

To you, you alone, O warlike Pallas Minerva, I pray, friend and companion in all my toils; Tamer of Lycurgus, ivy wreathed you crossed the eastern seas, bearing the Thyrsus in your hand; and you divine twins, Apollo and Diana, hear my prayer.
