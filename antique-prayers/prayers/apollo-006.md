## Оригинал





Apollo uti tibi in illis libri[s sc]riptum est quarumque rerum ergo quodque melius siet p(opulo) R(omano) Quir[itibus]
uti tibi VIIII popanis et V[IIII] libis et VIIII pthoibus sacrum fiat te quaeso precorque cetera uti s[up]ra.
Apollo uti te popanis dat[i]s bona prece precatus sum eiusdem rei ergo macte {h}eis libis libandis esto
fito volens propitius.

## Русский





Аполлон, как о тебе в тех книгах написано и ради каких дел — чтобы всё было лучше народу римскому квиритам,
чтобы тебе девятью попанами, девятью лепёшками и девятью фтоями свершена была жертва, — тебя прошу и молю; прочее как выше.
Аполлон, как я тебя, поднеся попаны, доброй молитвою умолил, ради того же дела почтён будь этими возлияемыми лепёшками
и стань благосклонен и милостив.

## Английский

Apollo, as it is prescribed for you in those books — and for this reason may every good fortune attend the Roman people, the Quirites — let sacrifice be made to you with nine popana, and nine cakes, and nine phthoes. I beg and pray that you may increase the sovereign power and majesty of the Roman people, the Quirites, in war and peace; as you have always watched over us among the Latins. Forever may you grant safety, victory and health to the Roman people, the Quirites. May you bestow your favor on the Roman people, the Quirites, and on the legions of the Roman people, the Quirites. May you preserve the health and welfare of the people of Rome, the Quirites, and may you always remain willingly favorable and propitious to the people of Rome, the Quirites, to the college of the quindecimviri, to me, to my house and household.
