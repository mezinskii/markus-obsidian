## Оригинал





tibi, frugum potens,
secreta reddam sacra: tibi muta fide
longas Eleusin tacita iactabit faces.

## Русский




## Английский

For you, Goddess of the Fruits of the Earth, your secret rites I will fund; in your shrine at Eleusis shall burn the sacred flame in celebration of your mysteries.
