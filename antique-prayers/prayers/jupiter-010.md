## Оригинал





Iuppiter omnipotens (nam te pernicibus alis
addere consilium volucresque implere futuri
ominaque et causas caelo deferre latentes
accipimus), non Cirrha deum promiserit antro
certius, aut frondes lucis quas fama Molossis
Chaonias sonuisse tibi, licet aridus Hammon
invideat Lyciaeque parent contendere sortes.

## Английский

Almighty Jupiter, You are He who imparts counsel to swift wings, and You who fills birds with foreknowledge of the future, and brings to light the omens and causes that lurk within the heavens. More enriched in mind is he, for whom You, O Dictaean Jupiter, announce Your will in the favoring flights of birds. May You allow that, guided by the skies, we shall have foreknowledge of the assembly of Argive forces and their initial movements in the fight ahead.
