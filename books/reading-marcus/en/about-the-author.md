Ian Mezinskii reads and comments on the *Meditations* of Marcus Aurelius. He created readaurelius.org, where the *Meditations* are discussed passage by passage, and where each passage is shown together with the Stoic disciplines, doctrines and terms it is connected with. He also writes a newsletter: each letter takes one passage of Marcus Aurelius and reflects on it unhurriedly.

[readaurelius.org](https://readaurelius.org) has a commentary on every passage of the *Meditations*, not only on those discussed in this book.
