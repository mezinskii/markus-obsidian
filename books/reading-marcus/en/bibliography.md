Listed here are the editions on which this book relies or from which it quotes. Where a quotation gives no translator, the translation is my own.

## Marcus Aurelius

- *M. Antoninus Imperator Ad se ipsum*. Edited by J. H. Leopold. Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1908. The Greek text against which this book has been checked.
- *The Thoughts of the Emperor M. Aurelius Antoninus*. Translated by George Long. London: Bell and Daldy, 1862. The translation in which the *Meditations* are given in this book, checked against the Project Gutenberg edition (eBook #15877).

## Other Ancient Authors

- Diogenes Laertius. *Lives of Eminent Philosophers*. Translated by R. D. Hicks. 2 vols. Loeb Classical Library. London: William Heinemann; New York: G. P. Putnam's Sons, 1925.
- Epictetus. *The Discourses of Epictetus, with the Encheiridion and Fragments*. Translated by George Long. London: George Bell and Sons, 1877. Quoted from the Project Gutenberg edition of Long's selection (eBook #10661).
- *Historia Augusta*. Translated by David Magie. 3 vols. Loeb Classical Library. London: William Heinemann; New York: G. P. Putnam's Sons, 1921–1932. Latin text consulted at LacusCurtius.
- Marcus Cornelius Fronto. *The Correspondence of Marcus Cornelius Fronto with Marcus Aurelius Antoninus*. Translated by C. R. Haines. 2 vols. Loeb Classical Library. London: William Heinemann; New York: G. P. Putnam's Sons, 1919–1920. The letters can be read at [readaurelius.org/fronto](https://readaurelius.org/fronto).
- Seneca. *Ad Lucilium Epistulae Morales*. Translated by Richard M. Gummere. 3 vols. Loeb Classical Library. London: William Heinemann; New York: G. P. Putnam's Sons, 1917–1925.
- Seneca. *On Leisure* (*De otio*). Cited by reference, without quotation.

## Pierre Hadot

- *Philosophy as a Way of Life: Spiritual Exercises from Socrates to Foucault*. Edited by Arnold I. Davidson. Translated by Michael Chase. Oxford: Blackwell, 1995.
- *The Inner Citadel: The Meditations of Marcus Aurelius*. Translated by Michael Chase. Cambridge, Mass.: Harvard University Press, 1998. French original: *La citadelle intérieure: Introduction aux Pensées de Marc Aurèle*. Paris: Fayard, 1992.
- *What Is Ancient Philosophy?* Translated by Michael Chase. Cambridge, Mass.: Harvard University Press, 2002.

## The Life of Marcus Aurelius

- Birley, Anthony R. *Marcus Aurelius: A Biography*. Rev. ed. London: Batsford, 1987.
